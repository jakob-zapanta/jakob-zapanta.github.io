In high school I was gifted a candle as part of a National Language Honors Society ceremony. The candle was more of a thing we used for the ceremony than a gift, but I wanted to keep it to represent my language learning journey. The only problem was, I had nowhere to display it. I wanted to display it so that it looked like the torches from minecraft, and I thought this would be a perfect application for 3D printing.

I have a bookcase in my room that I use to display awards, and I thought that the candle would fit in nicely here. The bookcase has holes on the inside of the walls for mounting shelves, and I realized that I could use these to mount my candle as well.

This part was essentially an extruded cylinder with a sharp angle cut out of one side, with walls on that cut side deep enough to accommodate two metal pegs. These pegs would stick out of the part, used for mounting the candle holder to the bookcase.
